FIDELITY RUBRIC v2 -- prompt form
=================================

Same five criteria the judge grades against, phrased as instructions so the
identical text can condition generation. Paste as FIDELITY_RUBRIC_BLOCK in
generator.py; keep in sync with tables/rubric_v2.tex in the paper and with
the rubric text in fidelity_judge.py.

v2 changes from v1:
  - "anachronism" REMOVED. It correlated 0.76 with combatants and 0.64 with
    material -- it was re-scoring those dimensions -- and 27% of what it
    penalized was accepted genre convention.
  - "objective" ADDED, tying fidelity to the playability level.
  - "pedagogy" RETAINED but excluded from the overall mean: it is 79%
    predicted by the other four (residual SD 0.32), the most derivative
    dimension in the rubric. It is still scored and reported, because it is
    the only criterion tied to the educational purpose of the work, but no
    reported comparison depends on it.

Overall score = unweighted mean of combatants, setting, events, objective.

--- block begins ---

HISTORICAL FIDELITY REQUIREMENTS

Your scenario will be graded on five criteria. Satisfy each one directly.

1. COMBATANTS -- the right people.
   Place the actual parties to this episode, and the actual commanders where
   history records them. If the engine ships a hero for a figure who was
   really present, use it. If it does NOT ship one, create a generic unit and
   RENAME it to the historical figure. Never substitute a different named
   historical hero: a renamed Spearman called "Abd al-Rahman" is correct,
   while placing SALADIN to stand in for him is a factual error about who was
   there. Match force composition to what the sources describe.

2. SETTING -- the right place and period, made visible.
   Assign every active player an explicit civilization consistent with the
   episode; do not leave civilization unset, because the game will otherwise
   choose the architecture, player names, and bonuses at load time and your
   scenario may render in an unrelated culture. Choose terrain, vegetation,
   and buildings that belong to the region and century.
   HARD REQUIREMENT: set player.civilization for each active player.

3. EVENTS -- the right story.
   Reproduce the shape of what actually happened, including the turning points
   that decided it and the real outcome. Do not invent reinforcements,
   battles, or speeches that did not occur, and do not misdate what did.
   Anything asserted in dialogue or objectives must be true of this episode.

4. OBJECTIVE -- the right problem.
   Make the victory and defeat conditions name the objective this episode
   actually posed, in the form the episode demands. Match the form to the
   situation rather than defaulting to elimination:
     - escort  -> BRING_OBJECT_TO_AREA on the escorted figure
     - defense -> survive to a deadline, or hold a named place
     - battle  -> DESTROY_OBJECT on the commander, or repel and survive
   A deadline is the CORRECT objective for a siege the defenders had only to
   outlast (Vienna 1529, Constantinople 1453); do not replace it with a
   kill-quest. Conversely do not use a deadline where the episode demanded a
   concrete act.
   HARD REQUIREMENT: never gate victory on an area count reaching zero
   (OBJECTS_IN_AREA <= 0), which becomes unsatisfiable if one unit garrisons,
   converts, or flees. Any other form is permitted; choose it by episode.

5. PEDAGOGY -- the right lesson.
   A player who finishes the scenario should come away with an accurate
   working picture of the episode. Objectives, dialogue, and events are the
   only channel through which they learn it, so put the true content there.

--- block ends ---

WHAT IS NOT PENALIZED
---------------------
The engine's inherent abstraction is not an error. Representing a Frankish
shield wall with the engine's Spearmen, or a Landsknecht with a Two-Handed
Swordsman, is a limitation of the medium and scores as correct. Only
avoidable error is penalized: the wrong person, the wrong culture, the wrong
events.

STATIC CHECKS (run before the judge; not the judge's discretion)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. combatants -- every placed named hero must be named in the episode brief.
   Tokenize the HeroInfo constant (dropping THE/OF/KHAN/HUN) and require a
   word-boundary match in title+description. Validated on the 31 built
   scenarios: flags 35 of 50 placed heroes, agreeing with the judge on every
   case. Necessary, not sufficient -- it does not check side, role, or
   whether a figure the brief requires is present at all.
2. setting -- player.civilization must be set for every active player.
3. objective -- EXTRACTION, not a pass/fail gate. Pull the structural form and
   target of every DECLARE_VICTORY trigger from the trigger graph
   (DESTROY_OBJECT / BRING_OBJECT_TO_AREA / TIMER / OBJECTS_IN_AREA, plus the
   target object or area) and hand it to the judge, which scores only whether
   that form fits the episode. Do NOT require a particular form: a timer
   victory is degenerate to the reachability audit but historically correct
   for a siege, so level 2 and level 3 may disagree by design.
   The only hard prohibition, shared with the audit, is OBJECTS_IN_AREA <= 0.
